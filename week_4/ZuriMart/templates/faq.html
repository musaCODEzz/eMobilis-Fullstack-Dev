Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping at ZuriMart.

🚚 How long does delivery take?

Deliveries within Nairobi take 2–6 hours on the same day. For destinations outside Nairobi, express delivery arrives within 24 to 48 hours.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, Debit/Credit Cards (Visa & Mastercard), and Cash on Delivery for verified addresses.

🔄 What is your return policy?

We offer a hassle-free 7-day return policy for any damaged, defective, or incorrect items with an immediate refund or replacement.

🛡️ Are products genuine with warranty?

Yes! Every item sold on ZuriMart is 100% original, verified directly from official manufacturers, and backed by a minimum 1-year warranty.
