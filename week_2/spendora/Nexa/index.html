How long does it take to integrate M-Pesa Daraja API into my website or app?

Once you have your Safaricom M-Pesa Paybill or Buy Goods Till number and administrator documents ready, our team typically completes and tests the STK Push (M-Pesa Express), C2B, or B2C integration within 3 to 5 business days, including going live on the Daraja portal.

Can you integrate our POS or billing software with KRA eTIMS?

Yes! We specialize in connecting existing ERPs, point-of-sale systems, and custom web applications directly to KRA's eTIMS (OSCU/VSCU) system so your business issues compliant tax invoices automatically in real time.

Do you assist with applying for shortcodes, USSD, and Bulk SMS Sender IDs in Kenya?

Absolutely. We handle both the technical development and the regulatory paperwork to register your custom alphanumeric Sender ID (e.g., YOURBRAND) and dedicated or shared USSD codes across Safaricom and Airtel networks.

Are your software systems compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act?

Yes. We build all platforms following strict encryption, consent management, and privacy-by-design guidelines aligned with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) regulations in Kenya.

Can we meet in person in Nairobi to discuss our project?

Definitely! Our team is based in Westlands, Nairobi. You can visit our office for a face-to-face consultation, or we can come to your premises anywhere within the Nairobi Metropolitan Area.
